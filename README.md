# PublicSupport
Develop a web page generic for use by non-profitable organisations to broadcast their public notices / advertising information about new products, special discounts, upcoming events and any other such distributable details. This avoids the wastage of brochure distribution costs, adverting p
fees for placing notices in magazines, news papers and other pblic media.

By providing the url for such important information for their customers. The customers can see from time on their personal devices such as Android tvs, mobile phones, and also at similar or related venues.

Typically Grocery merchants, travel agents, temples or community groups can reach their customer/consumer/member groups without incurring any addional costs. 

This can be broadcast from their servers to be presented at any intended user in a similar manner to the special news pop ups that we see during any news presentations.
